1. Project Overview
1.1 Introduction

HireFlow is a modern recruitment and job management platform designed to connect talented job seekers with employers through a centralized digital recruitment system.

Traditional recruitment processes often involve scattered job advertisements, manual CV screening, unorganized applications, and poor communication between candidates and recruiters.

HireFlow aims to solve these problems by providing a centralized platform where companies can publish job opportunities, candidates can discover and apply for jobs, and recruiters can manage the entire recruitment lifecycle from one dashboard.

Instead of being just another job board, HireFlow focuses on the complete recruitment workflow, from job publication to candidate selection and hiring.

1.2 Vision

To simplify recruitment by building an intelligent, transparent, and efficient platform that helps organizations find the right talent while helping job seekers discover meaningful career opportunities.

1.3 Core Objectives
Simplify Recruitment: Digitize and streamline the hiring process.
Centralize Job Applications: Provide one place for managing job postings and applications.
Improve Candidate Discovery: Help employers identify qualified candidates efficiently.
Automate Recruitment Tasks: Reduce repetitive administrative work.
Enhance Communication: Improve communication between recruiters and applicants.
Provide Recruitment Analytics: Give employers insights into hiring performance.
Support Career Development: Help candidates build professional profiles and track their applications.
